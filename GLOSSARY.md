# Chord Sheet Maker

A tool for writing, transposing and printing chord charts, and carrying them to the band.

## Language

**Song**:
One chart: its title, key, sections, lyrics and chords, and its named views.
_Avoid_: Chart file, track

**Setlist**:
A named, dated, ordered list of songs, each with its own key, capo and notes for that service.
_Avoid_: Playlist, set

**Library**:
One complete collection of songs and setlists, belonging to one band. Everything the song list and the Setlists screen show is the open library's contents.
_Avoid_: Database, workspace, account

**Share link**:
A link that carries a copy of one song, or of one setlist and its songs. Opening it adds that copy to the recipient's library; later edits on either side do not travel.
_Avoid_: Library link

**Setlist file**:
A file holding the same copy a setlist share link carries, for when the link is too long or unwanted.
_Avoid_: Backup

**Band**:
The people who share one library. Someone working alone is a band of one.
_Avoid_: Team, group, workspace, account

**Member**:
A browser that has joined a band, and so can see and edit its library.
_Avoid_: User, account, login

**Join code**:
A band's secret. Presenting it is what makes a newcomer a member.
_Avoid_: Password, PIN

**Invite link**:
A link that carries a band and its join code. Opening it joins that band, so everyone who does sees and edits the same songs and setlists.
_Avoid_: Library link, share link

**Recovery link**:
The invite link, kept by a member as the way back into their band after their browser forgets them.
_Avoid_: Password reset, backup

**Backup**:
A file holding every song and setlist in a library plus the device's settings, used to restore after loss.
_Avoid_: Export, setlist file
